TIER 3: the HEAVY WORKS (always on its big gear, meshing with a Mainspring) assembles PENDULUMS, which cut every enemy in the arc in front of them, and SPRING BALLISTAS; a NETTER's chain net (from the Light Works) stops one enemy for 4 s and brings a flyer down.

# Map sizes (0.15.0)

The owner asked, on 2026-10-28, first for Greenhold: "Scoot the spots a bit closer to the main and squeeze 2 more spots in this map." Then for every map. Then: enlarge all maps first, then place the base sites on the bigger maps.

## What changed

- Every built-in map is bigger. Most grow by about 1.3 on each side. That is about 1.75 times the ground.
- The classic maps go from 84 x 60 to 110 x 80. The walk from main to main is about 113 tiles. A Musketeer walks it in about 58 seconds.
- The team maps grow by the same factor.
- Some 1v1 maps already had a long walk. They grow a little less: Ashford Crossing by 1.2, Amberfall Vale by 1.21, Thornwood Maze by 1.16, and Saltmarsh Isles, Frostmere and Hollowfen by 1.15.
- Positions grow with the map. Widths do not. Ramps, rivers, fords and tree walls stay as wide as before.
- A main's plateau is one tile bigger each way. A main by the edge of the map keeps its place there, so its starting buildings sit as before. Its ramp and its natural keep their place next to it.
- Lakes and mesas grow a little. Forests grow with the map. The random woods grow with the ground.
- Every map has more base sites. They are the same for every player: the maps are still turned through their middle.
- Each side's natural is close to its main, in front of its ramp. Then come 3 or 4 more sites per side: corners, beside ponds and lakes, and pockets.
- No free site is within 24 steps of another player's main.
- Every site holds a hall's full reserves, for the Crown's farms and powder and the Clockwork's ore. Trees and decorations are cleared off each site and thinned round it.

## Greenhold

The owner drew it. For the top-left player:
- The natural moved up, to the foot of the main's ramp, on the left side.
- A new site in the bottom-left corner.
- A new site just right of the bottom-left pond.
- Two more: one along the top, right of the main, and one on the open ground below that.

The bottom-right player has the same, turned. The site right of the pond is close to the middle. The top-left player is about 72 steps from it, the bottom-right player about 80.

With 4 players each corner has its natural, right of its east ramp, and one more site on the open ground below it.

The other classic maps (Highmoor, Dune Sea, Frostfang, Twin Fords, Ashfall, Mirewood, Border March, Red Coast) share Greenhold's three builders. They got the same kind of sites. On the river maps the sites stay on your own bank.

Named maps that changed their natural:
- Ashford Crossing: the natural moved up, nearer the ramp (27 steps by land, was 29).
- Redrock Canyon: the natural is now right in front of the ramp, over the middle pass (24 steps). The old natural on the left is a third.
- Amberfall Vale: the natural is now right in front of the ramp (24 steps). The old one on the left is gone; a site near the top-left took its place.

## Old matches

- A replay of a built-in map from before 0.15.0 says its map was made bigger, and does not play. It was played on the old ground.
- A saved match of a built-in map from before 0.15.0 is not offered to resume, for the same reason.
- Your own maps keep their size. Old ones without a size still load at 84 x 60.
- A new map in the editor starts at 110 x 80. The editor's sizes now go from 64 x 64 to 256 x 256, with presets for every built-in size.

## Pictures

`docs/shots/map_sizes/<map>_before_after.jpg`: the old and the new map side by side, at the same scale (4 pixels a tile). `<map>_<n>p_before_after.jpg` is the same with every player. Every base site is outlined. White is a main. A coloured box is a free site, in the colour of the player whose main is nearest. A thicker box is a natural.

`docs/shots/expansion_spots/<map>.jpg`: the new maps with the gold expansion spots.

## Tools

- `tools/map_sizes.gd`: the table above. It fails when the sides differ, a site is right beside another player's main, a site is not buildable, or a main can't reach another.
- `tools/shot_map_sizes.gd`: the overview pictures with every site outlined.
- `tools/map_sizes_sheet.py`: the before and after sheets.
- `tools/scale_named_maps.gd`: the one-off helper that grew the named maps' data.
- `tools/propose_sites.gd`: proposes more sites for a map (open ground, room, spacing, on your own side).

# QA pass, 27 October 2026

The owner asked for two hours of testing everything and double checking all files and systems. This page is what was
checked on this machine; the cloud machines write their own pages: docs/qa_web.md (the web version in a browser, at a
computer's and a phone's size), docs/qa_soak.md (long computer games on every map size, save and continue, the sandbox,
the Clockwork lab, the map editor, replays, co-op online) and docs/animation_audit_<faction>.md (every animation).

## Every automated test

All 118 tests in tools/ were run on 0.14.53, two at a time (tools/test_*.gd; the online test separately, for each faction
as the guest). Four failed. None of them was a fault in the game:

- **test_barren_gather** was out of date. It spent the capital's ground to make a Feeding Pit barren, but since 0.14.42 each
  pit has its own ground and the capital none. It now spends every pit's own ground, and passes: grubs sent to a spent
  pit are told it is spent and none stays in its crew.
- **test_brood_fixes** was out of date in two ways. Its waits were lambdas reading the site variable, and in Godot 4.7
  the first of them read it as null after the `await` before it; they now take the site as an argument. And its Clockwork part placed a Spring Tower,
  which gear mode does not build; it now places the gear mode's supply building where its ring meshes (the game's own
  spot finder). All its checks pass: a Brood or Clockwork site whose worker is not coming any more goes, all refunded.
- **test_hotkeys** and **test_horses** fail only when they run beside other tests. Every test shares the game's one
  save file (user://), and test_hotkeys loads it back to check that control groups and camera views survive a
  CONTINUE: another test's autosave lands in between. Both pass when run alone.
- **test_start_layout** found one real unfairness: on Jade Wilds with 4 players, a Clockwork start puts its first
  building 9 tiles from the main building for players 1 and 3 but 7.5 for players 2 and 4. Every other map, faction
  and player count is mirror-fair. The cause: the four bases are of two shapes, two in the corners and two in the
  middle of the top and bottom edges. The start search (Game.find_start_spot) scores a spot by its distance plus 4
  for every free row between it and the edge, and for the Clockwork the Cog Press must also mesh with the Clocktower's
  gear ring. Against the top or bottom rim the press is too far for its ring to mesh (8.5 tiles); one row in from the
  rim it meshes, 7.5 tiles away, but scores 11.5. The middle bases have nothing better and take that spot. The corner
  bases also have the map's side rim in reach, where a press touching the edge meshes 9 tiles away and scores 9.0, so
  they take that farther spot. The Crown's Farm and the Brood's Feeding Pit have no ring to mesh and come out even. Two ways to fix it: let a spot one
  row in from the edge count as hugging it (the test already accepts that), or plant the missing trees on the corner
  bases' top rim. The first was done in 0.14.64 (Game._find_start_spot: a free lane to the edge costs nothing): the full layout test passes on every map (the farthest any start building moved from the old spot: 1.1 tiles, was 1.8), and the AI placement, rush, wall, pit ground, farm, gear and determinism tests pass.

The merges of the day were each tested on their own before they were pushed: replays (test_replay, the online test for
the three factions), the area marks (test_aoe_show, test_i18n, test_determinism), the Clock Soldier's shield
(test_clock_soldier_shield, test_west_views, test_anim_viewer) and the Clockwork sounds (test_clockwork_sounds,
test_alert_all). The Clockwork sounds test had to learn that a Clock Soldier now has 75 health: the one it had placed
first could fall in its own fights before it was needed.

## The balance study, early

The four study machines play much faster than estimated (about a game a minute each). Early numbers point one way:
the Brood is the weakest faction on today's rules. The Crown beat it in 37 of the first 41 games, the Clockwork in 14
of the first 20. At 5 minutes the Brood has earned about 60% of what the Crown has and has a third of its army. The full
report (docs/ai_balance_study_v2.md) comes when the 400 games are in.

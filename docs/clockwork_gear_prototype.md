# Clockwork gear prototype

The Clockwork's economy as a field of turning gears (`Defs.gear_mode`, default on). The old Line (rails, trains, pressure) is shelved behind `Defs.line_mode` (default off) and still works when switched on; the Line tests set `line_mode` true and `gear_mode` false (`tools/line_test_util.gd`).

## What it does

- **Gear field** (`scripts/gear.gd`, class `GearNet`): every Clockwork building has a ring of toothed ground gears, small or big; the Clocktower has the biggest. Teeth are a constant size (`pitch` 25 px: a 3x3 small ring has 21 teeth, big 30, the Clocktower 38), so rings mesh exactly: neighbours counter-rotate and the smaller gear spins faster. Replaces the blue field drawing.
- **Painted rings** (`scripts/gear_art.gd`, class `GearArt`): the ChatGPT rings rebuilt for any tooth count from strips (`assets/gear/`; reference `tools/gear_art/rebuild_ring.py`), baked once per (kind, teeth) into a texture (about 40-110 ms each on the dev box) and drawn flat on the ground: rotated, then squashed to `FIELD_Y`, with a thick edge (the silhouette 6 px deep). GearNet draws all edges, then all faces, then the placement ghost, on one node under the buildings.
- **Mesh rule** (`GearNet.mesh_phase`): tooth centres at phase + TAU*k/n; a child ring's phase is set every frame from its parent's so that at the contact a tooth faces a gap (u + v = 0.5). `mesh_error` measures it (tests).
- **Idlers** (`GearNet.idler_for`): rings snap to the tile grid, so a linked pair rarely touches. Within `mesh_tol` px they mesh directly; a wider gap (up to `slack`) gets a free idler cog of at least `idler_min` teeth whose pitch circle touches both, beside the line of centres on the side with more room. The ring then turns the same way as its parent. The ghost shows the idler it would get and turns already meshed.
- **Mainspring**: 3x3, winds TENSION (`players[t]["tension"]`, `Game.tension` / `add_tension`, wound through `GearEcon.wind`: the income multiplier applies, `stats["tension_in"]` counts it; shown in the top bar for the Clockwork only as held / stored); `Defs.GEAR.mainspring`: small gear 2.0/s with 3 Winders, big gear 2.6/s with 6 (the press crew logic). Every finished Mainspring STORES 200 Tension (big 400): winding stops when the pool holds what the springs store together (`GearEcon.tension_cap`; nothing unwinds, a refund may go over). A Mainspring also DRIVES the rings meshing with it (efficiency, below). Painted frames (`assets/buildings/mainspring_*`), working loop while linked, crewed and not full. Tension pays for the Jack, the Swift, the Clock Aviary and the research (tier 2, below).
- **Clockwork Lab** (`scripts/cw_lab.gd`): starts on the gear field with two Cog Presses and a Mainspring meshing round the Clocktower, Winders on them, the camera on it. Screenshots: `tools/shot_gear_lab.gd` (+ `tools/gear_art/frames_to_gif.py`).
- **Connection**: a BFS from finished Clocktowers over finished, living Clockwork buildings of the same team; rings link when the gap is within `slack`. Recomputed every 0.5 s (at once when the set of buildings changes). Unlinked buildings stop (`speed_mult` 0, production, repair and mends included) and show UNLINKED.
- **The level rule (0.14.28, owner)**: rings mesh only on the same ground level. A building's level is its footprint's height (`GearNet.foot_level` / `b_level`: `Game.height` 0 low, 1 high; a ramp is low ground and no building stands on a ramp or a cliff, so a footprint is on one level). Two rings of buildings on different levels never link, however close: not across a cliff, not along a ramp (the top and the foot of a ramp are two levels). And where two rings on one level meet, the ground is on that level too (`GearNet.joint_ok`): the contact point of a direct mesh, or one of the idler's two spots (`idler_spots`; `idler_for` takes the one on the level), so no idler sits across a cliff even between two plateaus. Applied in `solve` (the links, `spring_of`, the drive), `candidate` (the ghost says NO LINK: OTHER LEVEL and placing is refused with the reason), the % preview (`GearEcon.preview_steps`, `would_feed`), `ring_spot`, `GearEcon.room_spot` (the computer), the Lab and the study tool. Test: `tools/test_gear_levels.gd`.
- **Placement**: a new building's ring must touch the connected field on its own ground level (a Clocktower may go anywhere); overlap deeper than `overlap` tiles is refused (CLASH; rings on two levels may not overlap either). The ghost shows its ring and LINKS / NO LINK / NO LINK: OTHER LEVEL / CLASH / NEW FIELD (`GearNet.ghost_text`).
- **Constructs** wind down off connected gear ground (`Game.in_field`), as they did off the blue field.
- **Winder**: the only worker (speed 74, cost 50, 46 hp, 12 s); builds through the crate, repairs, winds.
- **Cog Press** (placeholder name, `Defs.GEAR_PRESS`): 3x3, replaces the Ore Crusher. Pays cogs a second: a 15% trickle alone, plus an equal share per Winder up to the crew cap, times its drive. Small gear: 8/s, crew 6 (a Winder adds 1.13, about a Labourer on a Farm). Big gear: 10.5/s, crew 12, better only from 9 Winders. (Crews were 3 and 6 until 0.14.28: docs/clockwork_economy_study.md.) Max 2 per Clocktower; each Clocktower holds a finite brass reserve (spent: a quarter). Crew assigned by the Crown farm crew logic.
- **Economy rules (0.14.28, `scripts/gear_econ.gd`, class `GearEcon`; the study: docs/clockwork_economy_study.md)**: DRIVE: the Clocktower and every Mainspring drive the field; a building meshing with one works at 100%, each ring farther from the nearest driver loses 10% (`Defs.GEAR.drive`: 100 / 90 / 80 / 70%); an idler is free; a Mainspring counts the other drivers only (`GearNet.drive_of`, rings from the nearest driver, filled by `GearNet.solve`; `GearNet.speed_mult` returns the drive share for a working building, so training and research follow it; `press_rate` and `spring_rate` multiply by it). Not for the Clocktower, Regulators, the Ratchet Turret (`GearEcon.DRIVE_FREE`). EFFICIENCY = crew share x drive x brass (`Building.efficiency` -> `GearEcon.efficiency`): the label over the building (crew, bar, %: `Game._draw_mine_labels`), the card's line (`GearEcon.card_text`), the placement ghost's verdict with the % it would have (`GearEcon.ghost_note`, `preview_steps`; a Mainspring ghost adds FEEDS n, `would_feed`). THE TENSION RULE, extended: anything that costs Tension is made only at a building whose ring meshes with a Mainspring's (`GearNet.spring_of` now holds every connected building's Mainspring; `GearEcon.spend_problem` in `Game.can_train` / `research_problem`): the Clock Aviary joins the Escapement in `Defs.TENSION_USERS` (idle without one), a Movement Works makes Jacks, Netters and Pendulums only so (`GearEcon.card_info` says which; the Pendulum also needs the Works' big gear). BUILDING TENSION PRICES: a `tension` key in `Defs.BUILDINGS` (the Clock Aviary: 150 cogs + 50 Tension): `Game.place_building` refuses and pays it, `abandon` / `site_failed` give it back (a begun site three quarters), the HUD shows `150+50` and the refusal, the computer pays it. The computer (`AI._drive_spot`, `_spring_spot`, `_jack_works`, `_air`; `GearEcon.room_spot` keeps room round a ring for the buildings that must mesh with it later): presses and Works on the Clocktower's ring, the first Works with room for a Mainspring beside it, the Mainspring by a Works (else by a Clocktower with room for an Escapement and a Works), a Works by the Mainspring when none may make Jacks, the Aviary by a Mainspring (else a second Mainspring first), Tension kept for the Aviary. For tier 3 it swaps a Works that meshes with a Mainspring to its big gear (`AI._tier3`). The Lab's Works stands on its big gear on the Clocktower's ring, and a second Mainspring hangs on it and feeds the Aviary; the Lab starts with its Mainsprings wound full (what they store). Test: `tools/test_gear_econ.gd`; screenshots `tools/shot_gear_econ.gd`; the study tool `tools/gear_econ_study.gd`.
- **Gear swap**: 25 cogs, 4 s, the building idles and the ring visibly grows or shrinks (`Game.swap_gear`, net command `swap_gear`).
- **Cogs**: the resource is renamed in the UI with a code-drawn gear icon, start 300.
- **No power (0.14.17)**: the owner dropped power ("steam and electricity are looks only"). In gear mode `Defs.ENERGY_KINDS` (Boiler, Charging Pylon, Engine Hall, Capacitor Works) are out of the build list, and `Defs.energy_only` keeps off every card anything that needs one or costs energy (Bulwark, Arc Thrower, the engines); `can_train` / `requirement_text` refuse them ("No power on the gear field."). The Clocktower fills no batteries and no longer zaps flyers; the top bar shows cogs, TENSION (`Defs.res_names`) and supply, no energy pill; the AI builds no Boilers; the sandbox CLOCK tab and the Lab leave them out. Line mode keeps all of it.
- **AI**: places presses, assigns Winders, and places buildings so they link; never builds unlinked ones.
- **Saves and online**: gear size and swap state are saved ("gb", "gs", "gst") and in `BLD_PROPS`; the guest recomputes the graph from synced buildings.

## Known gaps

- The Clockwork help text in the main menu (`scripts/main.gd`) still describes ore, crushers and rail vehicles.
- The AI swaps a gear only to put one Movement Works on its big gear for Pendulums (`AI._tier3`); its presses, Mainsprings and everything else stay on small gears (so it never builds a big Mainspring or a big press).
- The computer keeps room round its Mainspring only while it places it; a crowded plateau can still leave its Aviary waiting for a second Mainspring.
- Drive is worked out from the field's rings only: a building's % does not show for the enemy's buildings.
- The Regulator's (and the Spring Tower's) supply is not cut when it is unlinked.
- The plateau is small, so placement is tight; tune `overlap` (0.25 tiles now, about half a tooth) and `slack` (2 tiles).
- Only the parent link of each ring is meshed; two rings that touch without being parent and child can show teeth on teeth.
- The cogs icon, the swap button icon, the idler hub and the ring edge are code-drawn (see `docs/art_style_inconsistency.md`).
- Old saves with ore buildings are not migrated.
- The Winder's win and attack animations and death sheet are not imported.
- The Owl's falling bomb and a Netter's net in flight are not in saves (one in the air is lost; a unit already caught keeps its time).
- A netted flyer keeps drawing over ground units (its z order is a flyer's) while it lies on the ground.
- The Jack's leap is not in saves or online snapshots: a game saved mid-leap loads it where it was (popped onto free ground), and an online guest sees the Jack slide instead of crouch and fly.

# The new Brood (experiment)

The owner's redesign of the Brood, built as an experiment you can pick. The old Brood stays as it is and stays the default.

Where the decisions come from: docs/idea_brood_mobile_buildings.md (the owner's lines, in order). Code: scripts/brood_new.gd (the rules), scripts/ai_brood_new.gd (the computer). Art: tools/import_brood_new.py. Test: tools/test_brood_new.gd.

## How to play it

- In the skirmish setup, pick "The Brood (new, experiment)" in a slot's faction list. It is the last one.
- It plays as the Brood: same grubs, warriors, essence, sounds and music.
- Not in the online lobby yet. Replays and saved games work.

## What works

- No buildings. You start with the Brood Mother, one Rootmaw and five grubs.
- At the start, the Mother and the Rootmaw play the end of their rooting. Then they are rooted.
- Kill the Brood Mother and that side is out. Nothing else counts.
- ROOT and UPROOT for the Mother, the Rootmaw, the Mound Turtle and the Tyrannosaur. Rooting takes 4 seconds for her, 2.5 for the others. They always root facing south-east.
- Rooted: 3 more armour, then a quarter less damage. The Mother heals 4 a second while rooted. Rooted, the Mother spreads swamp 12 tiles round her (her main base, as the old capital), a Mound Turtle 10 tiles. A Rootmaw spreads none.
- NO ROOTED CREATURE ATTACKS (the owner, 15:58 for the Mother, 17:14 for all): rooted means building, for the Mother, the Rootmaw, the Mound Turtle and the Tyrannosaur alike; to fight they must uproot (Unit.can_hit). A rooted turtle on a ramp still body-blocks it; it just does not bite.
- Only the Mother lays eggs. She holds up to 10 and grows one every 6 seconds at first. THREE LEVERS raise her laying, and they stack (the owner, 17:39 and 17:55; the math in docs/brood_new_stages.md): her GROWTH STAGES (GROW on her card, rooted, no laying meanwhile: stage 2 for 300 food and 45 s, 15 eggs held, x1.3; stage 3 for 500 food, 75 essence and 60 s, 20 eggs, x1.7; bigger, slower, a wider swamp), each FATHER near her (+20%: pheromones), and each Mound Turtle rooted on NEW GROUND, 15+ tiles from her and from the other counted ones (+15%, up to 3). Turtles at home give supply and swamp only. Her card shows the levers ("Laying +76%: stage 2, Tyrannosaur near, 1 expansion"). The computer grows her when food piles up (from 4:30 and 8:00).
- THE MOUND TURTLE (the owner, 13:25, 13:34, 13:40) is the old Clutch Mound, the Brood's expansion and its tank: a huge swamp turtle a grub turns into (0.15.14; before, hatched from one of her eggs). Rooted, it sinks into the mud, gives 12 supply, makes her lay faster and turns the ground round it to swamp. Uprooted, it walks slowly and gives no supply and no swamp. It has a weak bite.
- Expansion: a Rootmaw roots only on its side's swamp, ANY swamp: the Mother's, or a lone turtle's far away (a hidden base). It need not be joined to hers. The farther from her, the more each grub trip pays, as before. Kill the turtle and its swamp shrinks back; a Rootmaw left off the swamp says "dry" and stops feeding.
- Body-block: a rooted turtle takes a 3x3 footprint on the grid (0.15.14, a building's; before, a 120 px body), so on a 3-tile ramp it closes it like the Crown's wall: nothing walks through, and since 0.15.14 its own side does not either (the footprint is solid for all, as a wall without a gate; the old "own side climbs over the shell" rule is DROPPED: a footprint that let one side through would need a gate's pathing, and the owner's ask was "a building in every way"). Uproot it to open the way. Flyers pass over. tools/test_brood_new.gd checks it on a real ramp: 8 Raptors, none past in 25 seconds; uprooted and moved off, all 8 go through.
- Its art is the owner's sheets (walk, rooting, rooted, uprooting, death); every walk direction is drawn (the north walk squashed to 78% of its height to sit as low and wide as the south walk; the north-east sheet's odd 5th frame dropped). Its card portrait is the owner's. Three walks and the portrait came as JPEG screenshots; how they are cleaned, and the check picture (docs/shots/brood_new/turtle_quality.png, tools/turtle_quality.py), are in docs/art_style_inconsistency.md.
- You pick a creature on her card (grubs and warriors only since 0.15.14). She lays its egg at once, rooted or walking. Walking, the egg drops behind her (the fall drawn in code). ROOTED, she lays it in her nest (the owner, 16:24, as the Town Hall's bell): her sheet brood_mother_rooted_lay_se_chatgpt.png, 6 frames lined up on the nest (it never shifts against her rooted loop) and scaled so her resting first frame covers as much as the loop's (the sheet drew her body a tenth bigger); frames 3 and 4 (her head reared high) are held longer (BroodNew.LAY_DUR, 1.26 s in all). The game's egg stays hidden until her last frame, appears exactly where that frame draws the new egg and as big (BroodNew.LAY_EGG_AT, LAY_EGG_BIG), then rolls out to the nest's rim and waits; her rooted loop has no extra egg.
- The egg hatches after the creature's normal time. It cracks for the last 0.8 seconds, then the creature stands on the open shell while it breaks apart.
- The Rootmaw roots on fresh ground on its side's swamp: 3,000 food in a 5-tile circle. Two rooted circles may not overlap. Old ground keeps what was left, like the old pits.
- A rooted Rootmaw drinks 4 food a second from its ground into its body. It holds 300. Six stages show how full it is, each breathing: the owner's five breathing sheets (rootmaw_breath_stage*_chatgpt.png, 3 frames each) were given to the stages of the original filling row they match best (shape and share of glowing food; tools/import_brood_new.py maw_stages): "stage2" -> stage 1, "stage1" -> stage 2 (its middle frame, with a big glowing ball a thin stage can't have, left out: a 2-frame loop), "stage3" -> 3, "stage4or5" -> 4, "stage6" -> 5. Stage 6 keeps its original still frame with the code breathing. The owner: "make do with this", no more redraws.
- Grubs take the food at its flank and carry it to the Mother. The farther, the more a trip pays, as with the old pits. Each Rootmaw takes 6 grubs, one more every 4 tiles beyond 6, up to 12.
- The grubs have their new harvesting animation at the flank.
- UPROOT spills the Rootmaw's food as a pile on the ground. Grubs can still collect it. It rots, 0.6 a second. The Rootmaw walks off thin and quick.
- HAUL uproots it full. It walks at half speed with its food. Rooted again, it keeps it. Killed while hauling, all its food bursts out as a pile. (Since 0.15.14 a move order never gets a rooted creature up: only UPROOT and HAUL do.)
- The Tyrannosaur is the father of the ground warriors: Raptors, Brutes, Spore Mothers and Mist Toads.
- The Tyrannosaur ROOTS (the owner, 15:45): rooted he is the Brood's barracks. He settles on a nest: the owner's sheet tyrannosaur_nest_se_chatgpt.png (rooting 5, a fertilising loop 6, uprooting 5), scaled so his standing first frame is as tall as his south-east walk (the same body size), every frame lined up on his green body and its feet, not on the nest (the last uprooting frame stands beside the empty mud ring). The loop's six frames hardly differ, so a light glow and flicker on the breath and eggs runs on top. Rooted he gets the rooted armour like the others; uprooted he walks and fights as before. A warrior's egg can only be laid while he is ROOTED within 7 tiles of the Mother.
- Her warrior eggs roll into his nest (BroodNew.nest_point: they are the red eggs drawn there, hidden until they hatch). Over him a count like a Barracks' queue ("3 eggs") and a bar for the first: its breath, then its incubation. He fertilises them one at a time (1.5 s each; the breath is in his picture, no code mist), they incubate and hatch in the nest. Warrior eggs left on the ground near him (laid before he rooted) roll in too. When he gets up, the eggs in his nest lie on the ground; never fertilised in 25 s, they hatch as grubs (the difference paid back). The computer walks him to a spot beside its Mother (three quarters of his reach out, clear of her nest) and roots him there; rooted too far from her, he gets up and follows; rooted he is not sent to defend (he bites from his nest).
- An egg he does not reach in 25 seconds hatches as a grub. The price difference is paid back.
- Only one Tyrannosaur at a time. A grub on its way to turn into one, or his cocoon, counts. If he dies, turn another grub into one.
- Essence: grubs harvest bodies to the Mother. SACRIFICE happens at the Mother (the owner, 13:25): the creature walks to her and she devours it with a swipe. Each offering also gives her 1 egg at once (the owner, 17:20; no age or type rule, the grub cycle is for playtests to judge); with her clutch full, no extra egg. There is no Brood Pen creature.
- THE MOTHER IS THE DEFENCE (the owner, 13:20): no tower, no guard creature. Since no rooted creature attacks (15:58, 17:14), tools/test_brood_new_defence.gd runs each early all-in two ways (9 Raptors, 3 Horsemen, 5 Musketeers, 12 Clock Soldiers, 2 Brutes with 6 Raptors; her numbers not changed). mode:uproot (she gets up and fights, as the computer does: BroodNew.mother_guard): she kills every attacker and lives, with 2,156, 1,381, 999, 886 and 921 of her 2,400 health left, in 15 to 74 seconds. mode:rooted (she stays in her nest): nobody dies on either side in 120 seconds; she is left with 2,177, 323, 38, 1,552 and 758 health, so against Musketeers and Horsemen she would fall soon after, and against the others only later. Rooted she is a wall that heals, not a defence: she has to get up. (Fixed on the way: an uprooted Mother ordered to fight used to root again by herself, the old "set up to shoot" rule for guns; the new Brood's rooters no longer do.)
- The computer can play it. It keeps the Mother rooted at home and, since no rooted creature attacks, gets her up when enemies reach her (strike at her or come within 3 tiles), fights on a 7-tile leash, and roots her again after 4 calm seconds (BroodNew.mother_guard). When enemies come near her it also gets the Tyrannosaur up from his nest to fight with the army, and roots him again beside her when it is calm; the Rootmaws and turtles stay rooted. It roots Rootmaws on fresh swamp round her, roots Mound Turtles round her for supply ahead of need, keeps grubs on them, lays the Tyrannosaur and roots him beside her, lays Raptors and some Brutes, and attacks in waves.

## Rooted = a building (0.15.14, the owner's playtest of 0.15.13)

The owner played 0.15.13 and asked that a rooted creature behave like a building in every way (docs/idea_brood_mobile_buildings.md, 19:19). Code: scripts/brood_root.gd (BroodRoot), with hooks in scripts/brood_new.gd, unit.gd, game.gd, controls.gd, ui/hud.gd. Test: tools/test_brood_new.gd section 15. Pictures: docs/shots/brood_structures/ (tools/shot_brood_structures.gd).

1. FATHER RANGE. While a Tyrannosaur (or a grub on its way to turn into one) is selected or his ROOT ghost is out, and while the Mother is selected, a ring round the Mother shows the father range (Defs.NB father_r, 7 tiles from her feet; "FATHER RANGE"). His ghost says IN RANGE or OUT OF RANGE; out of range is a red ghost (he can't nest there).
2. DRAW LAYER. Rooted (and in a cocoon), the creature's pictures hang under a node at the top of its footprint (BroodRoot.layer: Godot's y-sort sorts it there), so everything standing round it (grubs at the Mother's rim, Raptors) is drawn over it, as round a Town Hall; only what stands north of its footprint is behind it. No shadow ellipse on its body. Uprooted, it is a unit again.
3. COMMAND CARD. A selection of only rooted creatures and eggs gets a building's card: no MOVE, ATTACK, STOP, HOLD or PATROL (the A, M, P, S, H keys skip them too). Only their own commands: UPROOT (HAUL for a Rootmaw), the Mother's eggs, GROW and her levers, RALLY. Uprooted: the normal unit card, with ROOT.
4. STRUCTURE-UNITS FROM GRUBS (DECIDED). The Rootmaw, the Mound Turtle and the Tyrannosaur (BroodRoot.morph_list: later fathers too) are on a grub's card, not the Mother's (she lays grubs and warriors only; asked for one, she says "A grub turns into it now"). Picking one gives the ROOT ghost (point 8); PLACE sends the nearest selected grub: it walks there, pays when it arrives, and morphs into a COCOON on that spot: the old Brood's grub-morph strip, then the old Clutch Mound's construction mound growing, the creature fading in at the end (code stand-ins, docs/art_style_inconsistency.md). The cocoon takes the footprint at once, has health that grows like a building going up, does nothing yet (no supply, swamp, ground, fertilising); when it opens the creature is rooted there. The morph takes the creature's old egg time (Rootmaw 18 s, Mound Turtle 25 s, Tyrannosaur 35 s). One Tyrannosaur at a time counts the grub on its way and the cocoon. Blocked or unaffordable on arrival: the grub says why and stops (nothing paid). Shift-place queues more (each takes the next free grub).
5. GRUB CARD. A new-Brood grub's card has no old Brood buildings (no BUILD page; the B key shows nothing old): its build list is the three creatures above, plus MOVE, ATTACK, STOP, HOLD, WORK, HARVEST.
6. EGGS. A selected egg's card shows what it will hatch (its icon and name) and how: "RAPTOR · 12s", "RAPTOR · FERTILISED 9s", "RAPTOR · WAITING FOR THE FATHER 18s" (a tap or the tooltip tells more), and RALLY. Each egg the Mother lays takes her rally point; a right-click on the ground with eggs selected gives them their own; the hatchling walks there (a grub to the Rootmaw or food pile under the rally, if any).
7. RIGHT-CLICK. With rooted creatures (or eggs) selected, a right-click on the ground sets their RALLY POINT (a green flag and dashed line, as a building's). It never uproots: Game.smart_command takes them out of the order, and a move order given any other way (a hotkey, the computer) no longer gets a rooted creature up (Unit._tesla). Only UPROOT (and HAUL) does. The Mother's rally goes to her eggs and their hatchlings; the Tyrannosaur's to the warriors hatching in his nest (unless the player gave an egg its own).
8. ROOT LIKE BUILD. ROOT (on an uprooted creature's card) and the grub's creatures give a ghost on the grid: the rooted footprint, each cell green or red, the creature's rooted picture, and the reason when it can't: off the map, a cliff, trees/rocks/water, a building or a rooted creature, a building planned there, a ramp (only a turtle roots across one), uneven ground, another creature's planned spot, no swamp (a Rootmaw), another Rootmaw's ground circle (its circle drawn, and the others'), out of the father range (the Tyrannosaur). The creature walks there and roots on that exact spot (order "nb_root"; re-checked on arrival). Shift-place for several. ROOT where it stands (cmd_deploy, the computer's old calls) snaps to the grid and tries a tile to each side if its own cell is blocked. The computer (scripts/ai_brood_new.gd) uses the same checks (BroodRoot.place_problem, keeping its ramps clear) for its grubs' morphs, its Rootmaws' new ground and the Tyrannosaur's nest.
9. FOOTPRINT. Rooted, each creature's footprint cells are solid on the grid like a building's (Defs.NB "fp"; paths go round it; units standing there when it roots are pushed out, its own eggs excepted; uprooted or dead, the cells are free again; saved games put it back). Sizes: the Rootmaw 3x2 (a bit bigger than a Cottage's 2x2), the Mound Turtle 3x3 (about a Clutch Mound, and closes a 3-wide ramp either way), the Tyrannosaur 4x3 (a Barracks), the Mother 9x4 at stage 1, 10x5 at stage 2, 12x5 at stage 3 (her nest's ground, about the Clocktower's gear ring; it grows with her and pushes units out). Its edge is its reach (Unit.rect: grubs deliver at her rim, enemies hit it from its edge). A hatchling born inside a footprint (the father's nest) steps out at its rim toward the rally. The own-side pass over a rooted turtle is DROPPED (see the Mound Turtle above).
10. IDLE STILLNESS. Rooted, a creature animates only while producing (BroodNew.producing): the Mother laying, growing, or her laying timer running with room in the clutch; the Tyrannosaur fertilising; a Rootmaw drinking its ground or being harvested at its flank; a turtle never. Otherwise it holds its loop's first frame, without the code breathing or the glow's pulse.

Also in 0.15.14 (the coordinator's fix from the round-2 games, docs/brood_new_play_2.md): the computer's Mother (BroodNew.mother_guard) no longer flip-flops. She gets up when an enemy is at her (striking at her, or within 3 tiles), STAYS UP while any enemy is near (10 tiles of her body, or aiming at her from farther), and roots again only after 9 s of real calm, counted once she is fully up. The test: a Musketeer shooting her from range for 60 s gets her up once and never rooted again while it lasts; with it gone she roots after 9 s. The player's Mother never roots or uproots by herself (only the computer calls the guard, for its own Mother).

For the owner to know:
- The Mother's footprint: 9x4 at stage 1 is a little narrower than her drawn nest (about 11 tiles of rubble); wider would leave the Tyrannosaur no room to nest beside her within his 7-tile range except north and south of her. Easy to change (Defs.NB "fp").
- The cocoon is a stand-in (the old Brood's construction mound). A drawn cocoon per creature would look better.
- Paying on arrival (as the request said): a grub sent off can find the food spent meanwhile; it says why and stops.

## The numbers

- Brood Mother: 2,400 health, armour 1, speed 24, paw 40 damage every 2.4 s. Gives 16 supply, as the old Burrow.
- Rootmaw: 450 health, armour 0, speed 80 (40 hauling), bite 14. Costs 75, 18 s.
- Mound Turtle: 600 health, armour 2, speed 26, bite 6 every 1.6 s. Costs 100 biomass (150 until 0.15.15), 25 s, and the grub that turns into it (50). Rooted: 3 more armour and a quarter less damage, 12 supply, swamp 10 tiles; on new ground (15+ tiles from the Mother and other counted turtles) her eggs 15% faster (up to 3), a body 120 px across.
- Fairness of the turtle (docs/design_principles.md; the owner, 21:28, asked to justify the costs: a grub is used up when it morphs). Since 0.15.15 the turtle costs 100, and the grub it uses up 50 more: 150 in all for 12 supply, 12.5 biomass a point, the Crown's Cottage's 12.5 gold a point (100 for 8). Before, it was 150 plus the grub, 200 in all, 16.7 a point: the Brood paid a third more for its supply than the Crown. The same reckoning for the others: a Rootmaw is 75 + 50 = 125 against the old Feeding Pit's 125 (the old grub was used up building it too); the Tyrannosaur 200 + 50. The Crown closes a ramp with a Cottage and a Gate: 150 gold, 400 + 300 health, armour 1 and 2, and 8 supply. One rooted turtle closes it for the same 150 (with its grub), with 12 supply. It is harder to break than one Cottage (a Musketeer's ball does about 11 to it, 19 to a Cottage), but it is a single piece: the Crown's Gate falls in 17 balls, the turtle in about 55. It is slow (26) and gives nothing uprooted, so walling with it costs the Brood its swamp and supply elsewhere. Against small claws it is very hard (a Raptor does about 1 a bite): that is the rooted armour rule, the same for the Mother.
- Tyrannosaur: 1,000 health, armour 2, speed 64, bite 42 every 1.9 s. Costs 200, 35 s, 4 supply.
- Egg: 80 health, armour 1. Food pile: nobody's, cannot be attacked.
- The starting Rootmaw holds 120 food, so the grubs start at once.
- All numbers are in Defs.NB and the creatures' entries of Defs.UNITS. They are a first guess.

## Claude's choices, for the owner to confirm

- Supply: decided by the owner: the Mound Turtle. The Mother gives 16 (the old Burrow's), each rooted Mound Turtle 12, up to 200. Moving a turtle costs its supply while it walks.
- Essence and sacrifice: kept. Bodies and offerings go to the Mother.
- The old buildings' roles (the owner's, 13:20 and 13:25): the Burrow is the Mother, the Feeding Pit the Rootmaw, the Clutch Mound the Mound Turtle, the Spawning Den and Gorge Pit the Tyrannosaur, the Roost the Pterodactyl (later). The Brood Pen has no creature: SACRIFICE is at the Mother. Burrow upgrades: none; the Mother holds 10 eggs.
- Swamp (the owner's rules, 13:34 and 13:40): only round the rooted Mother and Mound Turtles. A Rootmaw needs it to root and feed. It still makes the Brood faster and heals it.
- Not in the new Brood yet: Spitters (their father comes later), Bloaters and Skywings (the Pterodactyl comes later). The Mother's card does not offer them.
- Research: none yet. The old Brood's upgrades lived in buildings.
- Unfertilised warriors' eggs: they wait 25 seconds for the father, then hatch as grubs.
- The Mother can be uprooted and walked. The computer does it only to fight off an attack at her nest.

# Clockwork Line: ChatGPT art prompts

Written 2026-10-25 from docs/clockwork_line_art_spec.md. Every prompt stands alone, with at most 3 reference images (in docs/redraw_refs/clockwork_line/). Save each answer as the sheet name shown, check it by eye, and tell Claude; the importer (tools/import_line_art.py) is built with the Line loader.

## 01_line_tiles

Save as `art/redraws/line_tiles.png`.

References: 3 refs: ref_boiler.png, ref_ore_crusher.png, ref_clocktower.png.

```
Create ONE pixel-art sprite sheet of straight and junction tiles for a steam main: a thick riveted brass pipe with a railway track laid on top of it, for a steampunk real-time strategy game. Canvas 1536 x 1024, landscape. Style: pixel art with hard pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients (dithering is fine). The look is the one of the attached reference images: a steampunk faction of brass, copper and dark iron, riveted plates, red painted metal panels, small gauges and valve wheels, soft light from the top left, dark navy-black outlines. View: a 3/4 view from above and in front (the camera looks down about 40 degrees), on a SQUARE world grid (NOT an isometric diamond). Background: flat navy RGB (0, 22, 48) everywhere there is no figure; no ground, no grass, no floor, no cast shadow other than a 1 to 2 pixel dark contact line. Do NOT copy the references' enlargement, labels or lighter boxes; use them only for style, colours and level of detail. Do not write any text, numbers, labels or cell outlines on the sheet. 
Layout: a grid of 5 columns x 3 rows = 15 cells. Each cell is a 256 x 256 pixel square and is ONE game tile drawn at 8x size (one game pixel = an 8 x 8 block of sheet pixels, so the art is chunky). Cells are separated by at least 32 pixels of pure navy, horizontally and vertically. Reading left to right, top to bottom the cells are: 1 straight pipe running EAST-WEST; 2 straight pipe running NORTH-SOUTH; 3 corner joining NORTH and EAST; 4 corner joining EAST and SOUTH; 5 corner joining SOUTH and WEST; 6 corner joining WEST and NORTH; 7 T-junction with arms NORTH, EAST and SOUTH (no west); 8 T-junction with arms EAST, SOUTH and WEST (no north); 9 T-junction with arms SOUTH, WEST and NORTH (no east); 10 T-junction with arms WEST, NORTH and EAST (no south); 11 cross junction, all four arms; 12 end cap with the pipe going only NORTH from the centre (a domed brass cap closes the south end); 13 end cap, pipe only EAST; 14 end cap, pipe only SOUTH; 15 end cap, pipe only WEST.
Design: the pipe is about half as thick as the tile (16 of 32 game pixels), centred in the tile, and meets the tile edge exactly in the middle of that edge, at the same height and thickness in every tile, with a slightly wider riveted flange ring right at the tile edge, so neighbouring tiles join seamlessly when placed side by side. On top of the pipe lies a flat steel deck carrying two thin steel rails on wooden sleepers; the railway follows the same shape as the pipe (it curves on corners, and has switch plates on T-junctions and the cross). Brass pipe with darker copper bands and rivets; a small red painted valve wheel only on the junctions (cells 7 to 11). Nothing may stick out of the 256 x 256 cell. All straight tiles must use the identical pipe and rail design; all corners and junctions use the same pieces.
AVOID: an isometric diamond view, a perspective that changes between tiles, missing rails, different pipe widths between tiles, smooth anti-aliased art, ground texture, shadows, text, cell borders, labels.
```
